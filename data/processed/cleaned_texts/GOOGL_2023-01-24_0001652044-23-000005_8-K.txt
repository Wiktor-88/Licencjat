Item 8.01. Other Events. On January 24, 2023, the U.S. Department of Justice, along with a number of state Attorneys General filed an antitrust complaint against Alphabet Inc. (“Alphabet”) and Google LLC (“Google”), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Alphabet, alleging that Google’s digital advertising technology products violate U.S. antitrust laws. Google believes the complaint is without merit and will defend itself vigorously.
