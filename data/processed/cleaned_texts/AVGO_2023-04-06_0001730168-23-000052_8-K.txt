(“Broadcom”) approved the amendment and restatement of the 2012 Stock Incentive Plan (the “2012 Plan”).  The Board of Directors of Broadcom had previously approved the 2012 Plan on February 6, 2023, subject to stockholder approval.  All executive officers of Broadcom are eligible to participate in the 2012 Plan.
