Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. On August 27, 2021 , Tesla Energy Operations, Inc. and The Research Foundation for the State University of New York entered into Amendment #12 to the Amended and Restated Agreement for Research & Development Alliance on Triex Module Technology (the “ Agreement ”), to memorialize an update of job and investment milestones required pursuant to the Agreement as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic.
