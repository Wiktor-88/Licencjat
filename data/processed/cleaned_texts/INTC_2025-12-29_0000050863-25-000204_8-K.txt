Item 3.02 Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities. On December 26, 2025, Intel Corporation (the “Company”) completed the issuance and sale of 214,776,632 shares of the Company’s common stock, par value $0.001 per share (the “Shares”), to NVIDIA Corporation (“NVIDIA”), for an aggregate purchase price in cash of $5.0 billion, representing a price per share of $23.28 per share. The issuance and sale were undertaken pursuant to a Securities Purchase Agreement (the “Purchase Agreement”), dated September 15, 2025, between the Company and NVIDIA, in a private placement relying upon the exemption provided by Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act of 1933 as a transaction not involving a public offering, as previously disclosed by the Company in a Current Report on Form 8-K filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on September 18, 2025.
