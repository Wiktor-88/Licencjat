Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders. The holders of Broadcom Inc.'s shares of common stock voted on the following matters at the 2022 Annual Meeting: (1) To elect the nine director nominees named in the proxy statement until the next annual meeting of stockholders or until their successors have been elected; (2) To ratify the appointment of PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP to serve as Broadcom’s independent registered public accounting firm for the fiscal year ending October 30, 2022; and (3) To hold an advisory vote to approve the compensation of Broadcom’s named executive officers. For each of these proposals a quorum was present. Each director nominee was elected and each of proposals 2 and 3 was approved by Broadcom's common stockholders.
