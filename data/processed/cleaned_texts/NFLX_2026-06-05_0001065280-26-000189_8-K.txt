Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders. On June 4, 2026, Netflix, Inc. (the “Company”) held its 2026 annual meeting of stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”). As of April 6, 2026, the Company’s record date, there were a total of 4,210,798,528 shares of common stock outstanding and entitled to vote at the Annual Meeting. At the Annual Meeting, 3,604,956,686 shares of common stock were represented in person or by proxy and, therefore, a quorum was present. For more information about the following proposals, see the Company’s definitive proxy statement, dated April 16, 2026. On May 29, 2026, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of the Company appointed Jay Hoag to serve as the Chairman of the Board, effective as of the conclusion of the Annual Meeting. Mr. Hoag previously served as the Board’s Lead Independent Director since 2012 and currently serves as the chair of the Nominating and Governance Committee. The Board determined it will no longer need a separate Lead Independent Director, as Mr. Hoag is an independent director under the applicable rules of the Securities and Exchange Commission and the listing standards of the Nasdaq Stock Market.
