Document Meta Reports Second Quarter 2024 Results MENLO PARK, Calif. – July 31, 2024 – Meta Platforms, Inc. (Nasdaq: META) today reported financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2024. "We had a strong quarter, and Meta AI is on track to be the most used AI assistant in the world by the end of the year," said Mark Zuckerberg, Meta founder and CEO. "We've released the first frontier-level open source AI model, we continue to see good traction with our Ray-Ban Meta AI glasses, and we're driving good growth across our apps."
